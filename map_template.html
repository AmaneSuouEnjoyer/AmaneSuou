Popular vote

How it is counted: in every state, each candidate gets the state’s electoral votes × their share of that state in percent. A 10 EV state where a candidate has 90% gives them 900 popular vote points. The percentages above are each candidate’s points out of all points, so they add up to 100%. The small numbers are those points.

Tied states: decided by a coin flip

    Margin of victory

    Margin is the gap between the winner’s share and the runner-up’s share of the state, in percentage points. For example, 60% against 40% is a margin of 20. Darker means a bigger gap.
